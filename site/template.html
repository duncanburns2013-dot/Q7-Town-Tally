Question 7, town by town

Question 7 would have required cities and towns to allow single-family homes on lots of 5,000 square feet or more. It lost narrowly statewide. The map shows how each of the 351 cities and towns voted, beside how its voters are registered.

Yes share against party registration

Each dot is one city or town, sized by Yes + No votes cast. Towns with more registered Democrats leaned Yes; towns with more Republicans or unenrolled voters leaned No. This is a town-level pattern and says nothing about how any individual voted.
